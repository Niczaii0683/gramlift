Questions people ask before ordering

Do you need my Instagram password?

No. We only need the username of a public account. You never share login details with us.

How fast is delivery?

Delivery starts within 24 hours of payment, usually much sooner. Larger packages are delivered gradually over a few days so the growth looks natural.

What happens if followers drop off?

Some natural drop-off is normal on any platform. If a large part of an order disappears shortly after delivery, email us with your order reference and we will look at it case by case.

Which posts can I boost?

Likes, views and shares are applied to one specific post or reel, so you paste that link at checkout rather than a username. The post needs to be on a public account.

Does the account have to be public?

Yes. Followers can't be delivered to a private account. You can switch it back to private once the order is complete.

Can I order for more than one account?

Yes. Add a package for each username to your cart and pay once at checkout. No account needed.

Is my payment secure?

All payments run through Stripe, the same processor used by companies like Shopify and Amazon. GramLift never sees or stores your card details.

What if something goes wrong?

Email with your order reference and we'll sort it out.
