HOW TO ADD PHOTOS TO GRAMLIFT
=============================

There is no upload button, and you do not need one.

This folder IS the upload. Drag a photo in here with the right filename
and it appears on the website. Replace the file with a different photo,
same name, and the website changes. You never open the code.

THREE STEPS, EVERY TIME
-----------------------
1. Rename your photo to one of the filenames below
2. Drag it into  Desktop/gramlift/public/media/
3. Open Terminal and run:
       cd ~/Desktop/gramlift
       git add .
       git commit -m "new photos"
       git push

Wait about two minutes. Done.

Any slot with no file is skipped automatically. Nothing breaks while a
slot is empty, so you can fill them one at a time.


THE SLOTS
---------

hero.jpg                 THE BIG ONE, top of the page
                         Landscape, about 1200 x 900
                         Someone on their phone works well.
                         With a photo here the layout changes: the photo
                         fills the space and the follower card shrinks
                         onto its corner. Without one, the card shows
                         on its own.

face-1.jpg               THE FACES ROW, just under the hero
face-2.jpg               Square, about 300 x 300
face-3.jpg               A face filling most of the frame.
face-4.jpg               Overlapping circles with a line of text beside
                         them. Add two or add four, both look fine.
                         None present and the whole row hides.

step-1.jpg               "WHAT ORDERING LOOKS LIKE"
step-2.jpg               About 900 wide, portrait or landscape
step-3.jpg               These are screenshots of YOUR OWN SITE, taken
                         by you:
                           step-1  package grid, username typed in
                           step-2  the Stripe payment page
                           step-3  the confirmation page
                         Empty slots show a plain numbered panel.

rotate-1.jpg             THE ROTATING STRIP, beside the reviews
rotate-2.jpg             Square, about 800 x 800
rotate-3.jpg             Changes every 2.4 seconds. Two files is enough
rotate-4.jpg             to make it rotate. Captions are edited in the
rotate-5.jpg             ROTATING list near the top of index.html.

review-1.jpg             REVIEW AVATARS
review-2.jpg             Square, about 200 x 200
review-3.jpg             Leave these empty until you have real customers.
                         A stock face next to a quote reads as fake.
                         Empty slots show a lettered circle instead.


EDITING THE WORDS
-----------------
Open  public/index.html  and press Cmd+F for  EDIT ZONE
Everything you would change day to day is in that block near the top:
prices, rotating captions, review quotes, the faces line, step text,
and the support email address.


FILE TIPS
---------
Use .jpg, not .png. Much smaller files.
Keep each one under about 300 KB so the page loads fast.
Free photos cleared for commercial use: unsplash.com and pexels.com
Search terms that fit: "friends smartphone laughing", "content creator
filming", "young woman phone cafe", "phone screen hands"
