Privacy policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy explains what information GramLift collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password when you create an account.
  • Order details: the Instagram username you enter, the package purchased, and the order reference.
  • Payment details: handled entirely by Stripe. We receive a confirmation of payment and the last four digits of the card. We never see or store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To deliver the order you placed.
  • To contact you about your order or respond to support requests.
  • To keep records required for accounting and fraud prevention.

Who we share it with

We share the Instagram username and package size with our delivery partner so the order can be fulfilled. We share payment information with Stripe. We do not sell your information to anyone.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and to honour the refill guarantee. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising trackers.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing support@gramlift.com. Residents of the EU and UK have additional rights under GDPR, and residents of California have rights under the CCPA; we honour those requests.
