Refund policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

30-day refill guarantee

Every package includes a refill guarantee. If the follower count from your order drops below the amount you purchased within 30 days of delivery, email us with your order reference and we will refill the difference at no extra cost.

When a full refund is issued

  • Delivery has not started within 72 hours of a completed payment and we cannot resolve it.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.

When a refund is not available

  • The account was set to private or deleted during delivery.
  • The username entered was incorrect. Please double-check it before paying.
  • Delivery has already started or completed.

How to request one

Email support@gramlift.com with your order reference (shown on the confirmation page and in your Stripe receipt). We reply within one business day. Approved refunds return to the original card within 5 to 10 business days.
