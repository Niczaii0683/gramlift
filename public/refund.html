Refund policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy covers orders placed through GramLift. Please read it before purchasing.

Check the details before you pay

Orders are sent to our supplier automatically as soon as payment clears, so they cannot be changed afterwards. Double-check the username, or the post link, and the quantity on the checkout page.

When we refund in full

  • Delivery has not started within 72 hours of payment and we cannot resolve it.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • The order failed on our side and could not be delivered at all.

When a refund is not available

  • The account or post was set to private, deleted, or made unavailable during delivery.
  • The username or post link supplied was incorrect.
  • The order has already been delivered.
  • Natural drop-off after delivery. Some decline is normal on any platform.

Significant drop-off

If a large part of a delivered order disappears shortly after arriving, email us with your order reference. We review these individually and will put it right where the fault is ours. This is handled case by case and is not an automatic entitlement.

How to ask

Email support@gramlift.com with your order reference, which is on your confirmation page and your Stripe receipt. We reply within one business day. Approved refunds return to the original card within 5 to 10 business days.
